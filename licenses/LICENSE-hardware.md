# Hardware license

The hardware design files in the `hardware/` directory are licensed under
the CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 - Strongly Reciprocal
(CERN-OHL-S-2.0). The full text is in `CERN-OHL-S-2.0.txt` in this
directory. If you modify the design and distribute products made from it,
you must publish your modified design files under the same license.

Source location: https://github.com/PionEars/IronEar

Covered files: schematics, bill of materials, printed circuit board layout
(KiCad sources), manufacturing files (Gerbers), and the enclosure model
(STEP). The schematics and bill of materials are published at campaign
launch; the board layout, manufacturing files, and enclosure model are
published when units ship.
